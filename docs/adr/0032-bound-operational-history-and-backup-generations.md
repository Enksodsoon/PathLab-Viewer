# Bound operational history and backup generations

Audit and Operations will expire application logs within 30 days, aggregated resource metrics within 13 months, Security Incident Records within two years, and authoritative Audit Records and governance receipts within seven years. The continuous PostgreSQL WAL archive retains every segment required from the oldest eligible daily anchor through the latest rolling seven-day PITR target: at least seven days plus the qualified daily-anchor interval of no more than 26 hours, with a hard maximum age of nine days; this explicitly supersedes the former seven-day WAL ceiling, while older discrete anchors retain only the WAL required to make that anchor independently consistent. Encrypted Backup Generations have a 35-day ceiling, disconnected generations lose their non-derivable per-rotation epoch DEK before that ceiling, Restore Drill reports have a two-year ceiling, and deletion-bound governed payloads become unrecoverable through per-object or common-retention-purpose DEK crypto-erasure when their final authorized generation expires even though deduplicated encrypted chunks or media remanence may physically persist.
