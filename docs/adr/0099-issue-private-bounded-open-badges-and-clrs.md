# Issue private bounded Open Badges and CLRs

Credential Ledger will issue and locally verify document-only Open Badges 3.0 and CLR 2.0 JSON-LD credentials using the exact frozen W3C VC v2, Open Badges context 3.0.3, CLR context 2.0.1, and Bitstring Status List v1.0 contexts and schemas. It fixes `DataIntegrityProof` with `eddsa-rdfc-2022`, RDFC-1.0, SHA-256, Ed25519 `Multikey`, `assertionMethod`, and signed revocation lists using `statusSize=1`, at least 131,072 expanded entries, and a canonical in-bound `statusListIndex`; it rejects undersized, out-of-range, malformed, decompression-bomb, remote, or unknown interpretation material. Only approved Course Completion and Assessment Achievement documents may be issued after Step-Up Authentication with opaque subjects, immutable supersession, at most seven years of validity or Institution custody, and no public Host, standard REST API, wallet, blockchain, paid-certification, or official-role claim.
