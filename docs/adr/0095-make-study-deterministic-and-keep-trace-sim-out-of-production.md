# Make Study deterministic and keep TRACE-SIM out of production

Existing Study Courses and Study Packs will be folded into Learning Catalog and Assessment, and the production Study journey will remain deterministic from immutable content, scoring, and approval rules. SmolLM2 is limited to Teacher Authoring drafts, while TRACE-SIM remains unapproved and excluded from production activation and release claims; removing its dormant implementation requires a separate approved change.
