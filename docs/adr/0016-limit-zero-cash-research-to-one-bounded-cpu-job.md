# Limit zero-cash Research to one bounded CPU job

Zero-Cash Production will qualify one active CPU-only Research workspace or reproducible batch job in an exclusive operating mode. Research Control grants immutable inputs and explicit CPU, memory, storage, runtime, and egress quotas to sandboxed Compute, which has no production database credentials, suspends after 15 idle minutes, and checkpoints or terminates safely on quota breach; funded profiles may add parallel or GPU workers without changing the control contract.
