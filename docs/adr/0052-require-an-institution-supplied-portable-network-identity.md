# Require an Institution-supplied portable network identity

Every production deployment will declare an Institution-supplied stable public subdomain or internal DNS name as its Deployment Network Identity. Caddy may obtain a public certificate through a qualified ACME provider or use an Institution-controlled internal certificate authority, with renewal and encrypted recovery of required certificate state tested; PathLab will not purchase domains or require DuckDNS, one ACME service, one public DNS operator, or another provider free tier.
