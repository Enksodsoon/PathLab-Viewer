# Require a 1,200-seat zero-cash Classroom launch gate

The Zero-Cash Production Profile will not launch until 1,200 synchronized Classroom participants pass the full 60-minute production evidence gate on the declared zero-cash host; timed Assessment has a separate 300-learner gate. Higher 1,500, 1,750, and 2,000 stages are breakpoint experiments only, and failure at 1,200 leaves the profile not qualified rather than lowering the launch requirement.
