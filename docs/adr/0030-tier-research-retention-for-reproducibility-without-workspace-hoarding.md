# Tier Research retention for reproducibility without workspace hoarding

Research Project authorization requires annual review; failed-job scratch expires within seven days, notebook checkpoints and temporary workspace state within 30 days of project closure, and workspace dataset copies within 90 days. Approved Research Artifacts, Environment Manifests, Dataset Snapshot manifests, and provenance may remain for seven years, while access and job-operation logs remain for one year; source datasets continue to obey their owning context rather than being preserved implicitly by Research.
