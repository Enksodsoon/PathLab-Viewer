# Require WebAuthn for privileged Memberships

Administrators and educators must complete password plus WebAuthn Privileged Authentication and enroll either two platform authenticators or one authenticator plus a sealed single-use Recovery Code Set. Institution OIDC remains an optional External Subject Mapping rather than a dependency, purchased hardware authenticators are not required, and local Break-Glass Grants require two distinct officers, expire after the named incident window, cannot bypass audit, and trigger credential review and rotation.
