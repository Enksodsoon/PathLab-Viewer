# Delete Clinical Shadow data when purpose expires

A Clinical Shadow Purpose Grant expires within one year unless explicitly reviewed and renewed. Rejected or quarantined input is destroyed within 24 hours and excluded from backups, accepted Clinical Shadow Records are deleted within 30 days after grant expiry, and Deidentified Snapshots follow their authorized destination schedule with a seven-year maximum; access evidence and Deletion Receipts may remain for at most seven years without retaining the deleted clinical content.
