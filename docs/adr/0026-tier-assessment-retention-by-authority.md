# Tier Assessment retention by authority

Assessment will expire Provisional Journals within seven days of confirmation or invalidation and abandoned drafts within 30 days of their deadline. Response Revisions and scoring evidence have a two-year Retention Ceiling after finalization, while confirmed Submission Receipts, final Grades, and grade-change history have a seven-year ceiling after course closure; Institutions may shorten each period and export official outcomes to their system of record before deletion.
