# Bound offline Assessment with renewable Attempt Leases

The Assessment server will issue an Attempt Lease valid for 30 minutes and refresh it every five minutes while connected. Disconnected work may continue only until the earlier of the server-authoritative Exam Session deadline or lease expiry, when the Provisional Journal becomes a Sealed Journal and the learner has 30 minutes to reconnect for integrity and timing validation; only the server may issue a Submission Receipt, and unresolved evidence routes to audited educator review rather than automatic acceptance, rejection, or grading.
