# Require independent brand and asset provenance

PathLab will ship an Independent PathLab Identity and an Asset Rights Ledger covering every image, icon, font, media object, and design source. The unproven `auth-histology.webp`, Mastercard or MarkForMC-derived guidance, copied trade dress, and any asset with unknown creator, source, license, attribution, hash, or permitted use must be replaced before release; repository presence is not rights evidence, approved fonts are self-hosted, and release admission fails closed on missing provenance.
