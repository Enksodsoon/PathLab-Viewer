# Tier EQA retention by governance value

EQA collaborative drafts have a 90-day Retention Ceiling after Round closure, distribution and access logs have a one-year ceiling, and Sealed Submissions, Scoring Versions, Appeals, and issued EQA Reports have a seven-year ceiling. Deidentified aggregate trend records may remain for at most ten years; Institutions may shorten every period, and retaining source-identifiable data under the aggregate category is prohibited.
