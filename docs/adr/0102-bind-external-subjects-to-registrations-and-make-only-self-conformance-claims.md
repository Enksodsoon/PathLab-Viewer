# Bind external subjects to registrations and make only self-conformance claims

An External Subject Mapping will key an asserted person by the external registration or issuer, `client_id`, and `sub`; `deployment_id` binds and authorizes a launch but never becomes the person's identity, email never merges records, and ambiguous mappings are quarantined. PathLab will run applicable official self-tests and independent conformance checks, but the Zero-Cash Production Profile will neither purchase nor claim paid 1EdTech certification.
