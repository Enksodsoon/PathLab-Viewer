# Require operated 90-day durability qualification

Every Zero-Cash deployment will name one trained primary and one alternate operator, keep synchronous WAL receipt continuously prioritized, and make full, incremental, object, prune, and scrub work yield to active learner modes without weakening the write-stop boundary. Launch requires 90 consecutive operated days on the actual production and Backup Target envelope, including two complete 35-day expiry cycles, RPO fault injection, restoration of the maximum actually admitted governed corpus, replacement-host restoration, disconnected-media, ransomware, and key-loss drills; signed campaign evidence remains portable across candidates only where its exact host, storage, network, configuration, control inputs, and expected result are unchanged, while every change receives a documented impact analysis and targeted reruns. The exact final release candidate must complete a 14-consecutive-day soak and all affected gates without reopening unaffected campaign days; that soak may occupy the campaign's final 14 days when the candidate remains unchanged, otherwise it restarts at the last candidate change. The primary data volume is a 150-GB raw encrypted volume whose admitted live corpus is necessarily lower under ADR-0045 and its 20-percent headroom, while a separate 150-GB portability and restore-throughput corpus runs only on capacity-qualified build/restore storage and is not a live-object allowance. Actual recovery time is recorded, but no fixed RTO or 24-hour availability promise is made.
