# Production Qualification

Production qualification binds evidence to one immutable release, one deployment profile, one host/resource declaration, one independent backup target, one client matrix, one standards corpus, and one campaign manifest. Reusing a historical result, changing a pin or host silently, or combining successes from different candidates is prohibited. The [Delivery State Ledger](./DELIVERY_STATE_LEDGER.md) defines lifecycle states and receipts; this document defines the gate register whose current results may be aggregated into a `PRODUCTION_QUALIFIED` receipt.

## Evidence and result model

Each gate emits exactly one `SUCCESS`, `PARTIAL`, `NEGATIVE`, or `NOT_EVALUABLE` result. `SUCCESS` means every frozen threshold, behavioral invariant, cleanup step, and recovery check passed on the exact subject. A missing artifact is `NOT_EVALUABLE`; an incomplete workload is `PARTIAL`; and an invariant or threshold breach is `NEGATIVE`. No result from another commit, host, profile, workload, or evidence window can fill a gap except unaffected long-duration durability evidence carried by a signed unchanged-input equivalence declaration. That declaration must prove identical relevant artifacts, configuration, host/storage/backup-target inputs, workload, operators, controls, and evidence schema; the exact final candidate still completes the mandatory 14-day soak and every affected or targeted rerun.

`CLINICALLY_QUALIFIED` remains a separate purpose-specific status. Base Clinical Shadow interoperability, self-conformance evidence, `PRODUCTION_QUALIFIED`, or `ACTIVATED` does not authorize diagnostic, patient-care, or clinical use.

## Cross-cutting failure campaigns

Every applicable gate includes:

- process, host and backup-target restart at pre-commit, post-commit/pre-delivery, and post-delivery boundaries;
- duplicate, delayed, reordered, missing and malicious events within the current-through-N-minus-two window;
- disk-full, inode, memory, CPU, database-pool, queue, bandwidth, storage-flush and clock pressure;
- expired, revoked, wrong-Institution, wrong-purpose and replayed Authentication Credentials, Adapter Credentials, Service Credentials, grants, tokens, keys, or leases;
- malformed, oversized, decompression-bomb, traversal, injection, hostile-document and unsupported-standard inputs;
- loss and rotation of service, signing, encryption, WebAuthn, node, adapter, backup-attestation and disconnected-media epoch keys;
- backup staleness, corruption, expiry failure and restoration from only off-host material; and
- deletion with caches, projections, exports, public-release warnings, Legal Holds, external Achievement Credentials, and deduplicated backup references.

## Evidence package

Each gate emits a sanitized, signed package containing:

- exact release commit, artifact digests, SBOMs, dependency/tool/model pins, schema versions, migration heads, configuration hashes, and applicable mitigations;
- host, OS/kernel, CPU/memory/storage/network/client and backup-target declarations without secrets or private addresses;
- frozen workload manifest, machine-readable behavior invariants, synthetic or authorized fixture identities, source rights, operators, and accountable approvers;
- start/end time, resource series, latency/error distributions, event/receipt ranges, retained raw evidence hashes, and invariant results;
- fault schedule, recovery path, cleanup, omitted or non-evaluable cases, and raw-to-summary provenance;
- the [Delivery State Ledger](./DELIVERY_STATE_LEDGER.md) subject and prior heads, result, expiry/review time, and exact invalidation rules;
- privacy confirmation that evidence contains no Authentication, Adapter, or Service Credential, PHI, private slide pixels, assessment answers, or participant-level telemetry; and
- independent technical-operator and accountable-Institution signatures, with two distinct approvers wherever separation of duty is required.

## Decision rules

- Any missing mandatory artifact or stale prerequisite yields `NOT_EVALUABLE`, never pass.
- Any frozen invariant, privacy, authority, data-loss, hard-resource, license, asset-rights, backup, key, security, clinical-boundary, zero-cash, or claim-boundary breach yields `NEGATIVE` and blocks qualification or activation.
- Passing only part of a workload yields `PARTIAL`; lowering a frozen threshold after observing the result is prohibited.
- `SUCCESS` requires every asserted threshold and invariant, including cleanup, deletion, and restore, with no disallowed waiver.
- Host, storage layout, database major, kernel/filesystem, key hierarchy, mandatory dependency/tool/model pin, protocol profile, client matrix, configuration, or authority-boundary changes invalidate the affected evidence and trigger targeted requalification.
- A route, unit test, synthetic fixture, deployment health check, historical campaign, or isolated context success cannot substitute for the complete vertical-slice and exact-release gates.

## Pilot and activation

After every prerequisite laboratory and exact-host gate plus the Golden Institution Journey succeeds, run a supervised limited pilot using approved non-clinical or already-deidentified material, bounded participants, and a named incident team. The pilot cannot replace load, restore, standards, security, privacy, or zero-cash evidence. Review every alert, support event, deletion, backup, cost, standards, and capacity receipt before Platform Governance aggregates the complete register into the Qualification Claim. Activation is a later, separate two-person operation that records the exact deployed qualified release, profile, host, evidence heads, accepted claims, expiry/review date, rollback target, and suspension conditions.
