# Bound Zero-Cash Edge Federation capacity

Zero-Cash Production will support 100 enrolled Edge Nodes while admitting at most ten concurrent control-sync sessions and two concurrent byte transfers. Each node may present up to 10,000 queued events and two GB of unsynchronized objects, and the Platform will reserve no more than 50 GB of total pending Edge bytes through the Storage Admission Ledger; work beyond either boundary remains under the authoritative Edge Node until a later reservation, while funded deployments may add workers and storage without changing the federation contract.
