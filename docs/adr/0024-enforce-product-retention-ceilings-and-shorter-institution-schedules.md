# Enforce product retention ceilings and shorter Institution schedules

PathLab will define and enforce a Retention Ceiling for every governed data class, while each Institution may select a shorter duration in its versioned Retention Schedule. Indefinite retention is prohibited; a Legal Hold must name its authority, scope, reason, start, and expiry, and every completed purge must issue a Deletion Receipt covering authoritative records plus known derivatives, caches, indexes, synchronization copies, and backup-expiry obligations.
