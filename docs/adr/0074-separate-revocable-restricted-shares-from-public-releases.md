# Separate revocable Restricted Shares from Public Releases

Imaging Control will expose Restricted Shares only to authenticated, purpose-bound audiences through private no-store responses and five-minute Tile Capabilities, with revocation effective within five minutes. Anonymous access requires a separately officer-approved Public Release after asset-level privacy and provenance review; its bytes use immutable content-addressed identities and may later be de-indexed at the origin, but PathLab will never claim it can erase copies already downloaded or held by an external cache.
