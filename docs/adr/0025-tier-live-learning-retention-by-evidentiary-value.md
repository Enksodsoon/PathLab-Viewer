# Tier Live Learning retention by evidentiary value

Live Learning will not persist presence, pointer, or viewport history; bounded connection diagnostics have a 30-day Retention Ceiling, Durable Interactions have a two-year ceiling after Class Session closure, and Attendance Intervals plus their minimum supporting evidence have a seven-year ceiling after course closure. An Institution may shorten each period through its Retention Schedule, while a valid Legal Hold remains the only permitted suspension.
