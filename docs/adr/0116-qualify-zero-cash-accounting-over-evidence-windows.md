# Qualify zero-cash accounting over evidence windows

Zero-Cash Production will count every PathLab-specific external cash obligation gross before credits, rebates, or promotional offsets, including cloud, licenses, APIs, support, newly purchased hardware, domains, utilities, and incremental connectivity, while separately disclosing donated or already-owned hardware, baseline utilities, and Institution labor. Launch requires 90 consecutive days of evidenced zero gross incremental charge and zero cash payment plus zero projected charge under the frozen workload for the next 12 months; the initial claim names that completed 90-day window and, once 12 months exist, the mature claim names the immediately preceding rolling 12 months. An expiring allowance or projected overage fails the gate, the Free Software Guarantee is permanent, and PathLab will never claim that operation is free forever.
