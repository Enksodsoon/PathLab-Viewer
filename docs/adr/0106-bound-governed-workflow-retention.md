# Bound governed workflow retention

PathLab will cap Authoring Draft retention at 90 days; Generation Records at one year after disposition; ordinary integration and quarantine payloads at 30 days; integration delivery receipts at one year; course Enrollment and Roster Snapshots at two years; published learning provenance at seven years; Edge conflict and rejection evidence at two years; and Credential Ledger status at seven years after expiry or revocation. The Clinical Shadow 24-hour quarantine ceiling overrides the ordinary integration ceiling whenever clinical material is involved.
