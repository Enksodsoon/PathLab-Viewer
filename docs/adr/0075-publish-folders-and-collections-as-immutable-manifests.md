# Publish folders and collections as immutable manifests

A folder or collection becomes publishable only through an immutable Collection Manifest enumerating every exact Source Asset and Browser Representation version, content hash, privacy decision, provenance decision, and audience. Each member passes independently, and adding, removing, replacing, or reprocessing an asset creates a new unpublished manifest requiring fresh approval; live-folder inheritance and silent expansion of an active Restricted Share or Public Release are prohibited.
