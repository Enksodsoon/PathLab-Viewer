# Keep DZI for browser delivery and standards for exchange

Zero-Cash Production will retain static or rebuildable DZI as its ordinary browser-delivery representation, while OME-Zarr 0.5 and DICOM WSI are validated Exchange Representations generated independently from the normalized source. Originals and signed provenance remain authoritative; PathLab will not claim generic lossless semantic round trips or silently flatten unsupported orientations, optical paths, focal planes, specimen facts, or other dimensions.
