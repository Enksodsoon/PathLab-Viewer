# Guarantee permissively licensed open-weight Teacher AI

Every distributed Teacher AI runtime, tokenizer, weight file, and conversion tool must have an OSI-approved software or artifact license, immutable source revision, complete notices, reproducible conversion recipe, hashes, and no fee or remote-service dependency. The launch model family will be described as permissively licensed open-weight AI rather than claimed to satisfy the OSI Open Source AI Definition unless separate training-data, training-code, parameter, and reproducibility evidence proves that stronger Model Openness Claim.
