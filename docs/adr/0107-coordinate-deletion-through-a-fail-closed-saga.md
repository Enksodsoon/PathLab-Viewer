# Coordinate deletion through a fail-closed saga

Trust and Governance will coordinate a fail-closed Deletion Saga in which every owning context returns a Deletion Receipt covering authoritative records, derivatives, indexes, exports, and backup-expiry obligations. Issued credentials are revoked rather than falsely described as erased, PathLab does not claim to erase externally downloaded Public Release copies, and a Legal Hold preserves live governed data or a separate encrypted hold package without extending ordinary backup retention.
