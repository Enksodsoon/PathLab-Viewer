# Seal EQA submissions and govern results and appeals

Two staff members may collaborate on an EQA draft, but one authorized submitter irreversibly creates the Sealed Submission and any correction proceeds only through an Appeal. Scoring is deterministic and versioned with human adjudication and no AI, a participant receives only its own EQA Report, aggregates with fewer than ten participants are suppressed, and appeals close 30 days after report issuance.
